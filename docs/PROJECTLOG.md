### ADR-060 — THE PRE-BETA DATA DOCTRINE: every firm's data is partner-authorized test data until beta; the agent holds full reset/reseed authority; the product's security mechanisms and the engineering gates are explicitly NOT relaxed (2026-08-06)
**Decision:** Owner-ruled (grilled 2026-08-06, four rulings): **(1)** The product has NO real clients today — every firm/client in the live DB (the ROME PROPERTIES / ROME SECRETARY corpora included) is **partner-authorized material for testing and review**. All "real data untouchable" prose across the harness (the honest-empty registers of ADR-056/058, RPR's nine reconciled months, the "never disturb" lines) converts to **resettable test state**: the agent may delete, reverse, reseed and re-run any client/accounting data on the live DB in service of wave validation, recording each reset in the session record. The earlier ADRs are not rewritten (append-only law); this entry supersedes their operational force pre-beta. **(2)** The authority is DATA-scoped only. The product's security MECHANISMS — forced RLS, the four structural invariants, maker/checker, audited-function-only writes, the wake allowlists — are **the product under test and are never weakened, bypassed or special-cased for testing convenience**; a test that needs a wall opened is a test of the wrong thing. **(3)** The engineering gates stay: `main` PR-only, green CI, the review ladders, cross-model gates where ruled — wave-validation quality is the point of this doctrine, not its casualty. Secrets discipline is unconditional. **(4)** The three raw corpora on the owner's desktop (BEE CREATIVE YA2024 · Rome Properties YA2025 · RS YA2025) are the sanctioned test material; where they cannot cover a mechanism, labelled-synthetic data remains the fallback (the ADR-048 sanction). **Expiry: this doctrine dies at beta/go-live** — the moment any genuinely un-authorized client's data enters, every suspended protection resumes at full force, and that resumption is itself a named gate item.
**Why:** The rebuild's validation was inheriting debt from a fiction — protections written for a live product were shielding partner-authorized test data from the very tests the partners authorized, and the "honest empty" deviations (correct under the fiction) were leaving whole mechanisms (real FA carry-down, payroll-shaped advances, real sales drafting) unproven against the only real-world documents available. Naming the doctrine, its exact boundary (data yes; mechanisms no; process no; secrets never) and its expiry converts an ad-hoc permission into an auditable law a future session cannot over- or under-read.
