# Research documents

Dated research from the 2026-09-08 refresh research phase, written by research agents working in
their own branches. The phase's **reports** were merged to `main` as the
`docs/plan/active/refresh-2026-09-08-*.md` family; the **documents those reports cite** stayed on
the unmerged branches and were published here on 2026-09-18, which repairs the citations in
`refresh-2026-09-08-kb-contract-evidence.md` and `refresh-2026-09-08-ux-flow-supplement.md`.

**These are dated research, not product law.** They record what was found on 2026-09-08 and some of
their proposals were explicitly *not* adopted, which the citing reports say in each case. The
enduring sources of truth are `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CONTEXT.md`; where a
research document disagrees with those, those win. Library versions quoted here are from
2026-09-08 and are not a current dependency statement.
