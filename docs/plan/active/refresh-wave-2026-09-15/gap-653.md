## 大白话

这张票要做的是：预付款（一年保险费、年度软件订阅）先按「预付资产」入账，然后依据一个**明确写下来的服务期间**，把金额按分精确摊到每个月，最后一期吃掉余数；每一期都能查、能改正，而且绝不能因为重试、掉包或重启就多跑一期。

今天已经有三块现成的料：(1) `0140` 建好了「服务期间」这张表、人工录入门，和一台已冻结、已被测试证明「精确到分、余数放最后一期、期间缺失就按名拒绝」的算式；(2) `0193`（#640）交付了完整的「授权计划」调度器——授权、修订、暂停、预览、补提、一期一次；(3) `0194`（#643）给出了「新会计操作怎么在不改冻结正文的前提下落地」的范本。

差在哪：这三块**没有一根线把它们连起来**。0193 明文按名拒绝 `amortisation`，而且它每一期的金额是常量——摊销天生每期不同。整个 `apps/web` 里除了两句「这里不建预付款计划」的文案，没有任何预付款界面。

**两轮审查改了三件要紧的事**：第一，那台算式**只出一半分录**（只有预付资产那一腿），判定费用科目、三道合规墙、以及「一分钱摊两个月会被截成零」的拒绝，全都住在另一个只服务 0045 旧车道的函数里——新门必须把这半边重新做一遍，所以工作量从 L 变 XL。第二，**AC5 的「停下来问」这一半根本做不出来**：开问题的那扇门只授权给 runtime、还必须拿着运行中的 hook token，人工门开不了；而冻结的提示词又禁止 Work 去读单据。第三，**计划每期失败是静默的**：拒绝只写在 occurrence 行上，没有 audit、没有 Work、没有通知——一条 24 期的摊销可以每个月失败一次，只有去翻计划详情页才看得见。

## Risks

1. **SILENT MONTHLY FAILURE — the largest design-and-safety hole.** `_plan_admit_occurrence`'s refusal arm writes only `accounting_plan_occurrences.outcome` and returns: no `accounting_work` row, no `clara._audit` row (the only one is `plan_occurrence_admitted`, `0193:1400`), no interruption, no notification (`0193:1371-1382`). A 24-month amortisation that begins refusing — locked period, deactivated authoriser, inactive client, lapsed egress — fails every month with the evidence visible only on the plan-detail projection (`0193:2098`). Without §E, the product's most common long-run failure mode is invisible.
2. **MODEL EGRESS IS A STANDING PRECONDITION FOR EVERY OCCURRENCE.** Each period is an ordinary `accounting_work` run; `claraWork_v3` prepares and consumes an authorisation immediately before the model call (`claraWork.v3.ts:10-19`), and `accounting_work` authority is derived from the firm's accepted Terms/DPA (`0195:502`). A superseded legal version silently stops every future occurrence of every schedule with a typed CLR13 — written nowhere a user reads.
3. **DOUBLE POSTING ACROSS TWO LIVE LANES — and the mitigation is weaker than assumed.** A firm can run a signed 0045 template carrying a `schedule` (`0140:660`) and a new amortisation plan over the same accounts. `_plan_overlap_warning` is called from `create_accounting_plan` (`0193:1552`) and `revise_accounting_plan` (`0193:1751`) **only** — so feeding the new kind into it does **not** cover a template signed *after* the plan, in either direction. Answering this properly needs a check at occurrence admission or on the 0045 sign door: a different and larger change. State the residual exposure in the brief rather than implying it is closed.
4. **THE EVALUATOR IS FROZEN AND UNGRANTED.** `prepayment_schedule_v1` is a registered single-member `evaluator_versions` closure (`0140:1180-1198`) hashed over the full `pg_get_functiondef` ignoring the `deployed` flag, and it sits in the closed ungranted census (`rig-meta.mjs:1203-1206`). Any formula change — including a "harmless" refactor — reds the apply; minting a grant to reach it reds the rig.
