## 1 · The ruled shape (fixed, not designable)

1. **The formal chain runs through the OBO lane and stops at issue** — open → evaluate → seal →
   render is Clara's, **issue is a human act** (TA-P14 (5); law 71's reserved list; E-R14). The
   contract's "end to end" is superseded; acceptance is written against four steps, not five.
2. **New authority arrives as wake SIBLING verbs; no live human body becomes dual-lane** (TA-P1's
   rider). Where both lanes must share one computation the split is the estate's idiom — **the body
   moves into a `(firm, actor, obo, wake_kind)` core and the public name is left a thin delegate
   with an unchanged signature** (`0069:340`, `0071:450-460`; F-A2's R-L3 move).
3. **Definition self-promotion is Clara's, with the machinery that makes it non-vacuous:** `approve`
   + `supersede` + **`reject`, including a human's draft** (TA-P1 C, F5-OQ-5's widest arm), plus
   **`create_account_set_v1`** and the **input snapshot mint**. `canonical` stays migration-only;
   statutory wording stays owner-signed (laws 71, 74).
4. **The issue-time segregation wall keeps biting.** The OBO lane writes the **DIRECTING human** into
   `requested_by`; the agent identity never occupies either end; a self-run pack **fails closed at
   issue** to a human who did not prepare; the receipt says **`agent_prepared`**, never `two_person`
   (TA-P6 A). The **solo arm** matches the close wall's CLR05 shape and auto-upgrades to
   distinct-checker when a second human joins.
5. **Typed reads for the formal chain; receipted reads for the sandbox** (TA-P4 A / F5-OQ-13). No
   table SELECT is granted to `clara_agent_ro` anywhere in this item (survey C4/C5).
6. **The sandbox exports freely** — watermark burned into the bytes, an export record, a mechanical
   recipient-coverage check, a versioned watermark row the owner signs once in three languages whose
   absence REFUSES the render, narrative-only aggregates (TA-P10 C-prime). **The EXPORT half is
   severed to its own design pass (§3.6)** — TA-P10 is not narrowed, it is handed to a design that
   can build it; the watermark policy family and the narrative-authority wall stay here.
7. **Monthly management packs self-run on the clock**, under law 71 and data gating, **exempt from
   `0084`'s orphan-adoption rule** (TA-P5 A + rider). **F-A4 mints the clock** (A4-M2); F-A5
   registers a consumer on it.
8. **The first real seal + byte-reproduction drill comes BEFORE N3's chart work**; every renderer
   image is retained seven years; renderer changes run as ceremonies (TA-P14's renderer clause).
9. **SST-02 is its own form producer sharing the deterministic evaluators — not a second
   architecture** (TA-P11 A (3)). F-A5 does not build it; F-A5 states the seam it must use.

### 3.2 The OBO closure — what 0077's refusal payload actually costs

`_eta_request_report_preview_core`'s `blocked_on` list is answered verb by verb:

- **open / assess / seal-dataset / seal-artifact / template publish:** each body's `_human_ctx(...)`
  line is replaced by parameters and the remainder moves verbatim into a core taking `(p_firm,
  p_actor, p_obo, p_wake_kind, ...)`; the public verb becomes the nine-line delegate `0071:450-460`
  models. **Six CoRs on live audited writers ⇒ the D1 window (§4)** — v1 counted three and declared
  the artifact core and the two template publishers off the list; the gate showed the artifact core
  is the **only** writer of the columns §3.3's wall reads (blocker 2) and that re-authoring the
  template publishers duplicates judgement `0069:261-266` forbids duplicating (material 9). The
  extraction is byte-preserving but for the context line and §3.3's identity writes — proven by
  **rig replay of the human lane before and after**, never by reading a diff.
- **evaluate:** not recut (S2) — **and not routed through `evaluate_metric_v1` either.** That
  entrypoint's own first statement is `c := clara._human_ctx(clara.role_rank('bookkeeper'))`
  (`0059:112`) and it is **ordinal 0** of the same frozen closure (`0059:246`): a wake credential
  carries `clara.wake_secret`, not `request.jwt.claims`, so the call raises CLR04 before doing any
  work — eta says so in its own words at `0077:369-375`. v1's chain had no middle (blocker 1).
  **The lawful entrypoint is eta's own:** `_agent_evaluate_fs_pack_core` resolves the deployed
  `evaluate_metric` v1 closure row itself (`evaluator_name='evaluate_metric' and version=1 and
  firm_id is null and deployed`, refusing `evaluator_undeployed` when absent), mints its own
  `metric_evaluation_contexts` + `_periods` rows, and calls the **frozen `_metric_eval_node_v1`** —
  `0077:160-164` + `:222-226`, the preview core's idiom. Cells stamp the **same**
  `evaluator_version_id` as `evaluate_fs_pack_v1`'s, so the assess and seal single-version checks
  (`0070:323-326`, `0070:502-505`) pass on a run a human started and Clara finished. **This is a
  SECOND cell-writing entrance and it is named as one** — TA-P11's review surface, and why C.4's
  census is written against the writers, not the callers. The orchestrator registers its **own
  appended** `evaluator_versions` row (`evaluate_fs_pack_agent`, v1), rostering its body and
  re-pinning the shared internals, and **never moves a member off** `evaluate_metric` v1's roster
  (survey C7) — the *"context-validated OBO evaluator core shipping as a new evaluator closure
  through its own ceremony"* `0077:377-378` names.
- **inertness is MECHANICAL, not believed.** The row is born undeployed and the flip is a ceremony
  act, never a migration act (`0060:93-100`; `0100:596-600`) — but nothing in the computation path
  read it, so v1's "the chain is inert until the flip" gated nothing (material 8; `0100`'s own
  resolver repoint is the precedent). So the core **resolves its OWN row first** —
  `evaluator_name='evaluate_fs_pack_agent' and version=1 and firm_id is null and deployed` — and
  raises CLR10 `evaluator_undeployed` when absent, in `0077:161-164`'s shape. A battery cell calls
  the verb **before** the ceremony and observes the refusal.
- **render:** the seal core lands **S9's missing line** — `perform clara._enqueue_render_job_core(
  p_firm, clara.agent_user_id(), p_obo, p_wake_kind, r.id, 'pre_sign')` for a claim-eligible pack,
  `'draft_watermarked'` for the preview path. The core is extracted from `clara.enqueue_render_job`
  (`0080:254`) because that body audits under `r.requested_by` (`0080:352`) — after §3.3's repair
  the DIRECTING HUMAN, so leaving it uncut would write "Alice enqueued a render" for a render Clara
  enqueued inside Clara's transaction (material 6: the S5 identity class, re-created in the audit
  trail). The public name stays the thin delegate the leader's fallback sweep (`0080:369`) calls,
  gaining only `prepared_by_agent`-aware attribution so the belt cannot re-create S5 either.

**TA-P11's one-architecture test, applied here.** One deterministic core (`_metric_eval_node_v1`),
one dataset recipe (`_report_dataset_payload_v1`), one claim assessor, one seal core, one geometry
library — **two entrances per surface (human door, wake door) is one architecture**; a second
computation of any reported figure would be two. Annex C.4 is the census that keeps that true.

### 3.3 The issue wall, re-armed — the sharpest cell in this item

**The rule.** Identity is recorded honestly AND the wall is re-aimed — doing only one created S5.

1. `report_runs` gains **`directed_by uuid references clara.users(id)`** (nullable) and
   **`prepared_by_agent boolean not null default false`**, both written at INSERT, so
   `_tf_report_run_lifecycle` needs **no** recut. *(**Trued at PR-1, and the ground is stronger than
   v2 stated.** The array at `0066:415-425` is not a "frozen-column list" — it is the list of seven
   columns **EXEMPT** from the freeze, subtracted from both sides of a WHOLE-ROW jsonb diff:
   `(to_jsonb(new) - v_frozen) is distinct from (to_jsonb(old) - v_frozen)`. New columns therefore
   join the frozen set **automatically**, and the trigger is `BEFORE DELETE OR UPDATE` — never
   INSERT — so a column written at INSERT cannot reach it at all. P6 CONFIRMED at the bytes.)*
   `report_artifacts` gains the same pair **and its INSERT is extended to write them**
   (`0071:432-435`, the estate's only `insert into clara.report_artifacts`): a column ARM 1 reads
   with no writer is a wall drawn and not armed — the exact S5 shape this section closes.
   **The artifact pair is DB-DERIVED from the run row, not taken from the caller** (ruling R-L23,
   F5-D32): every `pre_sign` artifact in production is sealed by `clara.complete_render_job`, so a
   caller-supplied pair would have left `report_artifacts.directed_by`/`prepared_by_agent`
   NULL/false on exactly the artifact the issue binds to. An explicit non-NULL `p_obo` that
   disagrees with the run refuses `artifact_identity_mismatch`. `_seal_report_artifact_core`
   therefore takes `(p_obo, p_wake_kind, p_agent)` **appended at the TAIL with NULL defaults**
   (F5-D32) and its `_audit` (`0071:438`) carries `(p_obo, p_wake_kind)` instead of `(null, null)`.
2. **`requested_by` carries the DIRECTING human when there is one.** The OBO core writes
   `coalesce(p_obo, clara.agent_user_id())` into `requested_by`, `p_obo` into `directed_by`, and
   `true` into `prepared_by_agent`. `wake_context()` has already re-validated that the director is
   an active bookkeeper+ (`0011:1147-1151`), so the value is a standing identity, not a stale one.
3. **The wall gains an ARM-0** (law 68 — the NULL/machine principal is its own FIRST arm, never
   inferred). `approve_report_for_issue`'s eligibility block becomes, in order:

   | arm | condition | outcome |
   |---|---|---|
   | **ARM 0** | `r.prepared_by_agent and r.directed_by is null` (a self-run pack) | the approver must be a human who did not prepare; **`c.actor = r.directed_by` is not evaluable, so the arm refuses `self_run_pack_requires_independent_issuer` unless `eligible_checker_count >= 1` and the approver signs the solo attestation** (§3.3.1) |
   | **ARM 1** | `eligible_checker_count(firm) >= 2` | refuse `report_issue_segregation_violation` when `c.actor` is **any** of `r.requested_by`, `r.directed_by`, `art.sealed_by`, `art.directed_by`; mode `two_person` when the pack was human-prepared, **`agent_prepared`** when `prepared_by_agent` |
   | **ARM 2** | `eligible_checker_count(firm) = 1` | the solo arm: an explicit self-attestation is required (unchanged from `0072:103-107`); mode `solo_self_attested`, or **`agent_prepared`** when `prepared_by_agent` |

   **Comparisons against a nullable identity use `is not distinct from`** (law 68's second clause):
   a CASE whose arms are all NULL-poisoned is an open door drawn as a wall.
4. **`issue_mode` gains `agent_prepared`** — an `ALTER TABLE clara.report_runs` CHECK swap
   (ACCESS EXCLUSIVE, D1), extending `('two_person','solo_self_attested')` to a third value and
   leaving the two existing arms byte-identical (extend-never-weaken). `ck_rr_solo_attested`
   (`0065:403-404`) is extended in the same swap so an `agent_prepared` issue also requires its
   attestation text. The receipt reads, in the owner's own words from TA-P6: *"prepared by Clara,
   sole human signer X"*.
5. **The hash binding is untouched.** `p_expected_artifact_sha256` must still equal the sealed
   `pre_sign` hash or the issue refuses `artifact_hash_mismatch` (`0072:87-92`); an agent-prepared
   pack does not relax it, and §3.9's issue card puts that hash before the human who signs.

**Why the agent is never simply barred from `requested_by`.** It is `not null` (`0065:379`) and a
self-run pack has no human to put there; writing a human who did not ask is a law-22 fabrication,
and leaving the agent there *without* `directed_by`/`prepared_by_agent` is S5. Recording both and
making ARM 0 read them is the only shape that is honest **and** biting.

#### 3.3.1 The solo arm, matched to the close wall

TA-P6 A routes solo firms through the existing CLR05 self-attestation arm (law 4; live-proven at
BEE), which `approve_report_for_issue` already has (`0072:102-107`). F-A5 adds that
**`prepared_by_agent` does not weaken it**: at `eligible_checker_count = 1` the single human still
signs, and the mode records `agent_prepared` so the audit reads truthfully. The wall **auto-upgrades**
when a second human joins — `eligible_checker_count` is evaluated at approval time, not stored.

### 3.4 Receipts — mechanically bound, and readable by a human (TA-P4 A)

**The constraint that shapes this:** `clara._audit` is a frozen closure member that **may not change
signature** (S8). So:

- **The binding half rides `audit_log.args`.** Every F-A5 core writes `_audit(firm, actor, obo,
  wake_kind, '<verb>', null, args)`, `args` carrying a typed `agent` object: `{model,
  model_version, rationale, wake_credential_id, wake_task_id, chat_turn_id, op_key}`. `actor`,
  `on_behalf_of` and `via_wake_kind` are **columns** (`0002:276-288`), not model text. **The
  completeness claim, stated exactly:** every audit row written by an F-A5 core carries
  `(obo, wake_kind)` — after §3.2's extractions that includes the artifact seal (`0071:438`) and
  the render enqueue (`0080:352`), both left hardcoded NULL by v1 under the claim "never NULL on
  this lane" (material 6). **The one reporting row that still carries NULL is the leader's fallback
  sweep** (`enqueue_missing_render_jobs`, `0080:369`) — a runtime belt, not a wake, enumerated here
  rather than papered over.
- **The judgement half gets its own relation.** `clara.report_agent_receipts` — one row per agent
  judgement act on this lane, append-only + no-truncate triggers in the estate idiom, carrying
  `report_run_id` / `definition_version_id`, the `agent` object above, the **typed outcome token**
  (Annex A.2), and the failing-rung vector where a ladder ran. Column list: Annex A.3.
- **No receipt, no act.** The receipt insert and the act are **one transaction** by construction:
  the core writes both, and the wrapper carries no DML, so there is no path that performs the act
  and skips the receipt. *(This is the DEFINER-wrapper property TA-P4 (3) asks for, obtained here
  without a separate wrapper because the act itself is already a definer core.)*
- **A human can read it.** A `bookkeeper+` read surface over `report_agent_receipts` — a PostgREST
  RLS policy pair in the `audit_log` shape — plus the `/reports` panel of §3.9. That read is itself
  not receipted (a human reading an audit surface is not an agent read).
- **F-A6's freeform reads** are receipted per read into `clara.freeform_read_log` (`0002:308-315`);
  **F-A6 owns that hardening, and its receipt must not be FORGEABLE by the payload, with the read
  role's privilege set over every other table unmoved** *(orchestrator ruling 2026-08-22 (R-L16):
  re-worded from "its receipt writer is never granted to the read role", unmeetable under F-A6's
  SECURITY INVOKER shape; it ships grant + one-arm/one-settle)*. Consumer: the severed sandbox item.

### 3.5 Definition self-promotion, and the trigger that has to move (S3)

- `_tf_metric_definition_lifecycle_v1` (`0059:26`) gains **one arm**: on `draft -> firm_approved`
  the evidence may be `{"kind":"human_approval","version":1}` **or**
  `{"kind":"agent_self_approval","version":1}`, the agent arm additionally requiring `approved_by =
  clara.agent_user_id()`, a non-blank `approval_reason`, `approved_formula_sha256 = formula_sha256`
  (unchanged), and non-null `approval_evidence->'agent'->>'model'` and `->>'rationale'`. **The human
  arm's bytes are untouched** — extend-never-weaken; the CoR is D1 #5.
- `_agent_approve_metric_definition_core` does not re-implement `0084`'s arms — it carries the
  **re-aimed** ones. v1 exempted the agent path from all four on the strength of TA-P5's rider,
  which exempts self-run report packs from **ARM 0 (orphan adoption) only**; standing law 69 says
  the opposite in terms — *"maker/checker measures the DIRECTING human … the same arms extend to
  any later act that mints-and-approves in one call"* — and `0084:13-16` names the exact hole in
  its own words: *"a human directs the agent to draft, then approves it. That is self-approval
  wearing a costume."* v1 re-opened it one lane over (blocker 3). **This is a WALL, not a human
  gate:** TA-P1 C's grant of the definition lifecycle is untouched, and Clara still approves her
  own undirected drafts. What the wall refuses is a HUMAN using Clara as his own checker. Arms, in
  order, with `v_maker` = the draft's human proposer or (for an agent draft) its
  `proposal_evidence.on_behalf_of` director re-read for standing, and `v_checker` = the approval
  wake's `on_behalf_of`:

  | arm | condition | outcome |
  |---|---|---|
  | **ARM 0′** | `v_maker is null` or the director is no longer an active bookkeeper+ (an orphan draft) | adoption with the attestation, **except inside a self-run pack**, which is TA-P5's rider at its stated width |
  | **ARM 1′** | `eligible_checker_count >= 2` and `v_checker is not distinct from v_maker` | refuse **`definition_directed_self_approval`** — the re-aimed `distinct_checker` |
  | **ARM 2′** | `eligible_checker_count = 1` and `v_checker is not distinct from v_maker` | the directing human's attestation text is required on the wake call (`p_self_approval_attestation`) and recorded on the receipt; absent it, refuse `agent_self_approval_attestation_required` (**OQ-5** — the owner may widen this) |
  | **fall-through** | `v_checker` is a different accountable human from `v_maker`, or `v_checker` is NULL on an undirected self-run draft | approved, `approval_arm='agent_self_approval'`, no attestation |

### 3.7 Self-run monthly packs (TA-P5 A)

A `reportPack.v1` workflow (new frozen export; law 40) woken by **F-A4's time-triggered wake

open→evaluate→seal→render and **stops** at `dataset_sealed` with a `needs_you` card for issue.
Design cell (TA-P5 requires one per new clocked act):

| what it checks | missing-data behaviour | who is notified | the hold |
|---|---|---|---|
