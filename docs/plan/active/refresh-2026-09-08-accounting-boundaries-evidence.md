## Scope and method

The accepted [Accounting Work contract](https://github.com/BELCORT-SDN-BHD/clara/issues/601#issuecomment-5581778992) supplies default autonomy, input with or without attachments, persistent work, no guessed settlement, current authority, locked-period handling and future-plan boundaries. Those choices are not reopened here.

Current repository traces are being checked separately against graph coverage and direct source, especially the latest SQL replacements. External references below establish domain distinctions or illustrate an API design; they do not select a Malaysian client's reporting framework or prove Clara's current implementation.

## Primary-source grounding

1. **Asset recognition and later depreciation are distinct concerns.** IFRS Foundation's IAS 16 overview separately addresses recognition, initial cost and subsequent depreciation/impairment. Its recognition discussion requires probable future economic benefits and reliably measurable cost. Design implication: a balanced debit to an asset account does not by itself describe all asset recognition facts or the later depreciation lifecycle. This note does not prescribe a client's useful life or decide Q19. [IAS 16 official overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-16-property-plant-and-equipment/).

2. **A changed estimate is not the same operation as fixing an error.** IAS 8's official overview distinguishes new-information estimate changes, recognised prospectively, from material prior-period errors and their applicable correction requirements. Design implication: policy/estimate edits need an effective date and classification; do not silently regenerate old postings from a changed field. Actual treatment depends on the client's applicable framework, period and circumstances; the owner's locked-period decision remains enforced. [IAS 8 official overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/).

3. **Payment identity and allocation are explicit business records in an established accounting API.** Xero's Payments API identifies the invoice, credit note, prepayment or overpayment to which a payment applies and the payment account. Its Overpayments API separately allocates an existing overpayment to outstanding invoices. These are implementation examples of why amounts, source transaction identity and settlement relations have different responsibilities; they are not a mandate to copy Xero's product or database. [Payments](https://developer.xero.com/documentation/api/accounting/payments), [Overpayments](https://developer.xero.com/documentation/api/accounting/overpayments).

4. **A journal can be an effect of several business operations.** Xero's documented journal source types distinguish invoices, their payments, credits, bank transactions, expense claims and manual journals. Design implication: a journal-result view can provide a common GL history while preserving the originating business action. This does not mean every metadata or matching operation creates another journal. [Journal source types](https://developer.xero.com/documentation/api/accounting/types/).

The current IAS 8 overview uses its updated title and describes its standards history. No standard-effective-date migration or reporting-framework adoption is authorised by this research. The Xero Manual Journals and Journals overview pages returned only JavaScript shells in this pass, so no additional claim is drawn from their unrendered contents.

## Owner answers and downstream contract

- Q19 accepted: when acquisition facts are sufficient, complete its posting and linked asset now; missing depreciation particulars remain pending with a question. Do not invent a method or claim dependent work is complete.
- Q20 accepted: remove the default human-only posting checker/attestation ceremony. Authorised human and agent accounting must preserve the same business meaning and consequences, with current roles, facts and accounting checks enforced. A direct action still records its business intent.
- Q21 accepted: an authorised human may explicitly accept individual missing-evidence readiness exceptions with reasons visible in the close result. Clara may continue when all remaining conditions hold, but cannot self-waive requirements or waive hard accounting identities. The exception does not silently become a standing waiver.

The owner's latest answer accepts these recommendations and emphasises agentic execution of the complete accounting outcome and its downstream impact. The target contract therefore requires identifying and resolving affected entries, open items, allocations, assets, plans, periods and report freshness; it does not grant authority to reopen locked periods, create unrequested future plans or rewrite issued report artifacts. Recoverable technical failures belong to the execution/retry mechanism, while facts and necessary decisions use the shared clarification interaction. Detailed acceptance scenarios are in the [product contract draft](refresh-2026-09-08-product-spec.md#21-业务操作与校验边界产品决策已确认待实现).

Required accounting invariants are not a preference vote: exact money, balanced GL effects, client/firm isolation, current authority, valid period, deduplication, source provenance and business-object consistency remain part of a correct accounting product.

### Entry-point parity and semantic gaps

- Client chat's draft/post tool currently requires a filed, verified document; the manual Journals composer submits an untyped, document-less JE. Both can reach shared approval consequences, but neither fact establishes the target no-document typed business instruction path. Sources: `packages/runtime/workflows/chatTurn.v13.tools.ts:257+`; `apps/web/lib/journals/api.ts:454+`.
- The raw-JE gap is semantic: an invoice entered with no business kind can become an adjustment open item. GL/subledger totals may tie while the business classification and due-date behaviour are wrong. This is a reason to preserve business intent through manual entry, not evidence that the database has no subledger enforcement.
- Zero-GL application is exposed in the direct counterparty UI; no direct chat/runtime `apply_open_items` tool was found in the bounded workflow search. Sources: `apps/web/lib/registers/counterparty-doors.ts:166+`; the SQL route above.
- Current bank chat exposes a bank-line-coupled settlement variant. No direct non-bank web/runtime adapter for the generic receipt/payment doors was found in the bounded searches. Their SQL alone does not prove the non-bank evidence flow is available. Source: `packages/runtime/workflows/chatTurn.v14.tools.ts:115+`.
- Fixed-asset particulars are exposed through the direct UI; no agent particulars-completion tool was found. Depreciation has a manual adapter and close-agent catch-up door, but that does not establish complete live client-chat reachability. Sources: `apps/web/lib/registers/fixed-assets.ts:162+`; `apps/web/lib/registers/depreciation.ts:128+`; `packages/db/migrations/0138_f_a4_pr_1c_close_agent_limb.sql:2345+`.
- Direct reversal exists, but no agent reversal tool was found in the bounded search. Reversal creates an unwind open item without automatically applying it against the original. The target complete correction must therefore also reconcile its outstanding-item outcome using the governed application/unwind operations as appropriate; simply producing a mirror JE is not sufficient completion. Sources: `apps/web/lib/journals/api.ts:342+`; `0037_wave_c_a_subledger.sql:920+,3316-3366`.

Avoiding duplicate settlement postings and completing the related state in a requested correction follow the accepted complete-operation/idempotency contract. They are implementation obligations, not new owner choices about whether extra manual steps or duplicate monetary effects are acceptable.

### Checks and recurring execution

- **Human approval ceremony is separate from accounting consistency.** The old human high-stakes branch includes attestation for agent-made drafts, a distinct eligible checker when there are multiple checkers, and solo self-attestation. The latest 0106 recut applies that branch only when `not is_agent`; agent posting uses the `agent_unattended` arm. Q20 is about that extra human ceremony, not permission, amount, period, revision or business-state checks. Sources: `0037_wave_c_a_subledger.sql:1992-2010`; `0106_f_a2_posting_core.sql:1304-1328,1496-1505`.
- **The thirteen agent rungs have different purposes.** They include untouched draft ownership, corroboration, evidence anchoring, amount tie, open questions and document/control-leg shapes. Several assume document-backed input. The current unattended-post verb therefore does not by itself implement the accepted no-document contract. The future common operation needs basis-specific evidence admission, rather than fabricating a document or removing all checks. Sources: `0106_f_a2_posting_core.sql:879-887,937-965,983+`.
- **Recurring execution already has deterministic authority and occurrence checks.** Templates are signed/live with immutable lineage; cadence, ended period, reservation, prior occurrence, rerun and collision checks constrain each run. The daily reconciler asks for the oldest due period and chains up to 24 occurrences per client in one pass, so missed periods can be caught up. Sources: `0140_f_a4_pr_2a_prepayment_limb.sql:2058-2179,2181+`; `packages/runtime/lib/reconciler-adjustments.mjs:20-49,139-241`.
- **Current recurring authority is stricter than the target default.** First occurrence and pre-signature catch-up remain drafts; only later low-stakes runs following an approved occurrence auto-post. This is current implementation, not an accepted requirement that every authorised plan needs a separate first-charge approval. Sources: `0140_f_a4_pr_2a_prepayment_limb.sql:2816-2846`.
- **Template change does not rewrite prior charges.** Replacement lineage is immutable; overlapping standing predecessor charges are refused and require correction or a forward start. Preserve effective scope and posted history when revising plans. Source: `0140_f_a4_pr_2a_prepayment_limb.sql:2662-2691`.
- **Control fields encode necessary business identity.** AR/AP control legs require a counterparty. A human generic control entry produces an adjustment open item, whereas the current unattended generic lane refuses control legs. Unifying input paths must preserve intent and detail while exposing an appropriate typed adjustment operation; neither unrestricted raw control writes nor copying the current agent restriction is the complete target contract. Sources: `0106_f_a2_posting_core.sql:625-637,1199-1213`; `0037_wave_c_a_subledger.sql:990-1027,1071-1080`.
- **Prepayment arithmetic is substantive, not another model opinion.** The current proposal uses a posted source with one prepaid-asset debit, a recorded service term and exact-cent allocation whose residual is placed in the final period. Service-period facts are superseded rather than silently edited. Sources: `0140_f_a4_pr_2a_prepayment_limb.sql:576-611,1032-1085,1111-1155`. Reuse the exact arithmetic where applicable; an agent can decide the evidence-based term and handling, but it must not invent a term or change past recorded charges.
- **Explicit plan instruction and first-occurrence review are different.** A sufficient authorised instruction can supply standing scope; current separate admin-signature/proposal and earned-ramp machinery is an implementation constraint, not a new product automation mode. An explicitly requested past start still needs clear catch-up scope, open periods and sufficient basis; asking for a future plan does not imply retrospective charges. Sources for current proposed-template/signature flow: `0140_f_a4_pr_2a_prepayment_limb.sql:3629-3661`; `0045_wave_d_b2_recurring_adjustments.sql:4264-4348`.

### Close requirements and report outcomes

- The checked migration catalog contains 15 checks: 7 absolute accounting identities, 6 readiness exceptions that may be attested under the existing policy, and 2 advisory views. Sources: initial catalog `0056_wave_e_close_model.sql:390-406`; undated-document addition `0104_f_a4_pr_1a_measurement_layer.sql:622-624`; deferred-opening addition `0161_qd6_close_seal_wall.sql:406-419`. This is a catalog count, not a claim that all underlying evaluator implementations were line-audited.
- The hard identities cover AR/AP/FA ties, bank identity, retained-earnings roll, opening continuity and deferred opening. Readiness covers depreciation through year end, stock, drafts, bank gaps/exceptions/enrolment and uncoded/undated codeable documents. A default-agentic close still needs these applicable conditions; it cannot substitute its own attestation for failed requirements. Q21 settles the human exception policy separately from these hard identities.
- Current closing retains human-only capability and segregation/self-attestation boundaries. The accepted autonomous target therefore needs a correctly attributed agent completion path while retaining the accounting checks. Source: `0128_f_a4_pr_1b2_a4_truth.sql:142-146,235-297`.
- P&L roll and opening continuity are represented as literal pass objects before finalisation even though the dry-run labels them not measurable and finalisation performs the actual checks. Preserve the final checks, but distinguish deferred validation from a verified green readiness result. Sources: `0104_f_a4_pr_1a_measurement_layer.sql:428-477`; `0128_f_a4_pr_1b2_a4_truth.sql:299-369`.
- The catalog applicability is limited to `always` and `goods_trading`; document gates inspect existing documents. The checked path does not establish Q18's client-specific expected-source coverage, so never-filed mandatory evidence needs a versioned requirement and measurable missing state. This is a bounded finding about that close path, not every possible document integration.
- Finalising close records the P&L transfer, receipt/snapshot and closed FY state. Formal reporting has a separate evaluate/seal/render lifecycle. A reporting artifact is an output linked to the work, not the definition of close or a reason to duplicate a closing JE. Sources: `0128_f_a4_pr_1b2_a4_truth.sql:372-497`; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:221-238`.
- `close_gate_results` has no explicit evaluator-version field in the checked definition. Version the effective requirement/evaluator meaning before changing checks, and preserve prior measured results. Source: `0056_wave_e_close_model.sql:467-498`.

Do not generalise the existing successor/effective-date mechanisms into a rule that every accounting-policy change is prospective. IAS 8 distinguishes policy changes, estimate changes and corrections; the applicable framework and facts determine the treatment. The engine must preserve past recorded history while representing a required correction/restatement explicitly, including the owner's locked-period decision.

### Coverage limits

Current-source traces use codebase-memory discovery and coverage checks plus direct reads for large/partial SQL and full-migrations searches of the named current definitions/dynamic recuts. All cited SQL was partial and metadata changed; web scopes had no recorded parse gaps and runtime workflows had unrelated one-line gaps. Root additionally read the FA birth, zero-GL application and latest human/agent branch text directly. These signals are best-effort, not completeness proof. No tests were run for these facts, and source-level availability does not prove live chat tools, worker enablement or hosted acceptance. The underlying AR/AP/FA ties, bank-identity, depreciation and stock evaluators were not each audited line by line; their complete correctness requires representative datasets and execution evidence. Owner Q19–Q21 are accepted; this evidence note does not mark the implementation complete.
