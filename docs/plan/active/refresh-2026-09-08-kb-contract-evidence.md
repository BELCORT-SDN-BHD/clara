## External format check

The [format recheck](refresh-2026-09-08-kb-format-recheck.md) confirms the maintained OKF repository moved to `GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format`; the current spec remains v0.2. Karpathy's ingest/query/lint and progressive wiki discovery inform the maintenance design. OKF supplies a portable representation; its conformance and trust labels do not supply Clara's runtime, authenticated review, tenancy or write authority. See the [canonical specification](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format/blob/main/SPEC.md) and [author's LLM Wiki note](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f).

## Engineering decision and handoff

Use one governed knowledge service and product surface over existing typed facts/identities and source history, with rebuildable wiki/OKF views. Keep identifiers and relevant actor/source/effective versions, and progressively retrieve relevant content for the same Clara harness. Model-generated experience is useful context, not a new permission system. Imported declarations remain source claims until the corresponding Clara checks support them.

Do not inherit obsolete human binding ceremonies as universal prerequisites, nor bypass old functions by pretending the agent is a human. Introduce the needed attributed capabilities and preserve equivalent identity/accounting checks. A company with both vendor and customer roles can have linked knowledge without collapsing AR/AP accounting roles or netting its open items automatically.

Migration must account for legacy merges without lineage, kind-scoped writers, proposal-only identity automation, the narrow fact catalog/admin-only write door, the split Knowledge/Reports surfaces, pack failure and freshness, incomplete source/claim metadata, omitted identity-change events and projection dead letters. Exact tables, tool signatures, indexing strategy, backfill, cutover and rollback remain implementation design. The accepted behaviour is testable without assuming Google Knowledge Catalog, a new vector service or a particular agent platform has already been adopted.

## Verification limits

Two bounded source lanes used codebase-memory discovery, exact path/scope coverage, current-definition searches and direct reads. All cited SQL had partial parse coverage; checked paths reported metadata changes. The root independently read the source-page recut, `msic` seed, current v17 preload path, derived coding patterns, canonical merge-read edits, alias-kind FK/trigger and proposal-only agent binding boundary. Negative claims are limited to inspected migrations and production runtime/web literal references; hosted state, external consumers and dynamic names are not excluded. This is not a whole identity/knowledge audit.

A separate lane checked primary format sources and reviewed the proposed contract against Q10/Q22/Q23 and the accepted autonomy boundaries, finding no material inconsistency. No business tests, full migration replay or hosted accounting actions were run for this decision. Local documentation receives a consistency check; implementation and deployment evidence belong to the later slices.
