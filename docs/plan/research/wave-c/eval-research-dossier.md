## 4. Domain reality — Malaysian bank statements, SME reconciliation semantics, 'reconciled to the sen'

BANK STATEMENT FORMATS (MY, from knowledge — flag as NOT primary-verified, confirm with owner): PDF monthly statements are UNIVERSAL across Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, RHB, Hong Leong, AmBank, Bank Islam, OCBC, HSBC, UOB, BSN. CSV/Excel export is available via business internet banking (e.g. BizChannel@CIMB, Maybank2u Biz, PBe biz) but coverage/columns vary by bank and product tier. MT940 (SWIFT) is generally an ENTERPRISE cash-management feature, NOT typical for the SME clients Clara targets — do not assume availability. Realistic v1 ingest = PDF via the existing OCR pipeline (proven: bank_statement@0.99, Gate C) + CSV where a client has it. Statement columns typically: date, description/narrative (bilingual BM/EN, truncated), debit, credit, running balance; cheque/reference numbers; opening + closing balance. OCR hazards: multi-page continuation, running-balance drift, merged debit/credit columns, encryption (Gate C found a bank-export PDF's encryption tripped the Slice-5 intake quarantine, ADR-030 lesson c).

SME RECONCILIATION SEMANTICS: (a) MATCHED — statement line ↔ existing approved GL bank-leg entry (a booked payment/receipt). (b) BANK-ONLY / not-yet-in-books — bank charges, interest, standing-instruction debits, FPX/DuitNow fees, auto-debits: these become NEW coding tasks (book Dr charge/Cr bank), not force-balanced. (c) BOOK-ONLY / timing — unpresented (outstanding) cheques and deposits-in-transit: in the books but not yet on the statement; carry forward as outstanding items. (d) At takeover these are the Wave B opening_items bank_uncleared rows (item_ref = cheque/instrument no, item_date = instrument date) that Wave C's matcher clears as they appear.

'RECONCILED TO THE SEN' (the eval criterion): the bank reconciliation statement ties EXACTLY — GL bank balance ± outstanding lodgements ∓ unpresented cheques == statement closing balance, zero residual. A non-zero difference is an EXCEPTION (an unbooked charge, a mis-match, a missing line), NOT a rounding plug — this contrasts the ≤5¢ journal-rounding auto-post (PRD:122) and must be an explicit owner ruling. Per-LINE match may permit a small tolerance (ARCHITECTURE:88 'amount-beyond-tolerance') for e.g. an absorbed bank charge, but the PERIOD tie-out should be exact. The DB owns the reconciliation figure (read from a recon read-fn), the agent never computes it. Cross-FY reality (F12-2): year-end cheques clearing in the new FY MUST reconcile after the prior FY is closed — the textbook case the prior build could not handle.
