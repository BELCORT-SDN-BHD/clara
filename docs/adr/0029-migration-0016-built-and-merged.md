### ADR-029 — Migration 0016 BUILT + MERGED via blind lanes + a six-round cross-model ratchet; execution-truth ratified over review claims (2026-07-23)
**Decision:** Wave A2.1's DB heart is **MERGED to `main`** (PR #55, `711cc88`) — migration `0016_a21_compliance_watch.sql` implementing the contract's P1–P7 pins: the non-blocking per-group SST compliance watch (completed-month statutory test on **Asia/Kuala_Lumpur** dates, earliest-crossing, sticky liability, human-attested future method folded at the earlier statutory test), the sales-autopost lift (structured + the nine-control OCR envelope on a **single bound extraction**, credit-side sightings, supersession genealogy implemented), the doc-type classifier gate (classify-first facts dispatch, unforgeable human kind-precedence via the source marker), the purchase SST visibility split, `closing_transfer` (draft + revise), context-pack v3, and **in-migration audited live-books repairs** (customer/AR vendor rules, out-of-bounds rules, non-canonical aliases — nobody-wins collisions), prestate-fixture-proven against a staged 15-migration DB. **NOT yet deployed** (live stays at 15 migrations until the ceremony). En route it closed a **pre-existing privilege escalation** (`sign_coding_rule` had no rule-type guard — a bookkeeper could sign an autopost rule live) and hardened `add_client_alias` + the runtime matcher to one byte-equivalent normalization (**matcher-v2**).
**Why (recorded lessons):** (a) **The blind-lane structure earned its cost**: the contract-blind battery (74 cells, written from pins alone) caught a real CHECK NULL-leak the implementation lane missed, and its 25 documented pin ambiguities became binding adjudications instead of silent divergence. (b) **The cross-model ratchet took six rounds** (13 findings → 5+5 → 7 → 3+4 → 1 → RESOLVED) and the early rounds were load-bearing: a CRITICAL structured-rule envelope bypass, a migration-aborting repair BLOCKER, session-timezone statutory dates — same-model review had rated the tree sound at round 0. Cross-model adversarial review before merging money-touching DB work is confirmed house law, and **six rounds is now twice the observed convergence count** (A2, A2.1) — budget for it. (c) **Execution truth outranks read-only review claims**: the reviewer could not run the batteries and twice asserted coverage gaps the lane refuted with actual runs (and once the lane's own pushback was refuted by the catalog — both directions matter; the rule is *evidence, not authority*). (d) The 83-test stale sweep held the line on "update the assertion only where a PIN sanctions the new behavior, with the citation in-file" — zero unsanctioned regressions rode in. Ref: PR #55, `docs/plan/wave-a2.1-migration-0016-design.md`, the adversarial memos in the session evidence, ADR-028.
