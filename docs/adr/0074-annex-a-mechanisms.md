#### TA-P1 — the orchestrator's dissent, in full (moved verbatim; the ruling itself stays in 0074)

*ORCHESTRATOR'S DISSENT — option A, the two-question rule — ON FILE.* A recommended a decision RULE rather
than an open register: of each unnamed act ask **(i)** does it make a difference, an obligation or a named
question DISAPPEAR from the books, or make something bearing the firm's name effective outside it (a
certificate, a published template, an issued pack)? and **(ii)** is it STRUCTURALLY IRREVERSIBLE (bytes
sent, a permanent row minted, a human's signature voided)? Either yes → human; both no → Clara. **The costs
stated, in full and in session:** **voiding a reconciliation destroys a segment of reconciliation history
with no human eye on the act** — what is lost is a certification, not a posting, so reverse-not-delete does
not recover it; and **registering a bank account binds an account to a COA code, so a wrong binding
reconciles an entire month into the wrong account while the screen still reads "reconciled"** — the silent
wrong number, which hard constraint 1 ranks above everything else. The dissent priced its own cost equally:
under A the 60-day waiver stays human, real books carry over-60-day open items most months, and unattended
reconciliation would routinely stop and wait. **Heard, costed, declined.** Rider (3) is the wall the
duplicate-payment concern earns inside the owner's ruling.

#### The 2026-08-22 human-act delegation on test data

Recorded here because it changes who may perform the acts this sitting names. **On TEST DATA the agent may
perform law-71 human acts as the owner's delegate**, through the **real audited doors** — never a bypass,
never a synthetic principal — with each act **ledgered**. **E-filing is excluded by its nature**: an external
statutory submission is permanently a human act. **Every act on real client data remains the owner's.** The
practical companion to ADR-060's DATA-scoped authority: the mechanisms under test are never weakened; only
the hands on them change, and only on test data.

**R-OWNER — raised AFTER the sitting by the F-1 verifier; RULED 2026-08-22, option C, INTO PR-1.**
**B15's second door.** A supplier bill STATING a registration that is untestable because the client file holds
neither TIN nor SSM resolved to `none` (`0049:986-988`) — and `0049:975-979` records that real Malaysian
clients typically hold an SSM and no TIN, so this was the common case — the generic arm PASSED and **GB-1's
phantom-payment shape landed through D18's door.** **RULED: the ROOT fix, and it rides PR-1** (not a later
migration): **`_document_direction`'s testability is recut so ONE held hard id suffices** — the stated
registration is compared against **every** id the client holds, and a stated id of a kind the client has not
recorded yields a NEW evidence class, **`untestable`**. It joins **PR-1's D1 list with a `prosrc`-SHA pin.**
**Orchestrator rider under the standing delegation (walls validate, fail-closed): B15 ALSO refuses
`generic_registration_untestable` when a supplier registration is STATED but untestable** — a client holding
no hard id at all, or the new `untestable` class. **D18 now stands for direction-SILENT documents only.**
