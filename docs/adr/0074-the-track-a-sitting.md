### ADR-0074 — THE TRACK-A SITTING: fourteen principle rulings for F-A3..F-A9; the roster becomes an OPEN REGISTER; attribution becomes JUDGEMENT (2026-08-22)

**Fourteen principle rulings (TA-P1 … TA-P14), minted in one structured-question sitting on the day F-A2's
PR-1 build opened. The input was a Track-A question collection over F-A3…F-A9 (66 questions; an independent
review kept 59, refused 7, named 34 further omission candidates), compressed into fourteen questions each
answerable from the contract plus standing law alone and each settling its member questions by logical
consequence — the ADR-0071/G1-G9 shape. The agenda as put to the owner is preserved verbatim, member tables
included, at `docs/plan/active/track-a-sitting-1.md` · `-2.md` · `-3.md`; this entry minutes the rulings and
points at it. Every option carried its cost stated in advance and a fail-closed silence default; every
declined build recommendation is recorded as declined, and the orchestrator's two dissents (TA-P1, TA-P7)
are on file below with their cost arguments in full — dissent-then-execute.**

**FOUR CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS FALL OUT OF THIS SITTING. RATIFIED 2026-08-22 (owner).** Drafted below
as proposed wording and entered the ratified set at **the owner's digest sign-off**, as laws 68-77 did:
(i) law 71's *exactly*-enumerated human roster becomes an OPEN REGISTER (TA-P1); (ii) invariant (a), client
attribution, becomes the agent's judgement under structural walls (TA-P7) — amending PRD §6.2(a) **LAW**,
ARCHITECTURE §0.1 and digest law 2; (iii) law 21 narrows to periodic POSTING belts (TA-P5); (iv) law 76
drops the drifted "LLM" qualifier (TA-P13). **No product text is edited by this entry** — that stays true.

#### The rulings

**(TA-P1) Who holds an act law 71 did not name? — RULED: C, THE OPEN REGISTER.** *The question:* law 71's
human list says "exactly", yet dozens of acts are named on neither side and are human today only because a
human was the only thing that existed when they were written.

**RULED: every act law 71 does not EXPLICITLY reserve is Clara's.** The seven reserved acts stand unchanged
and are the whole human half: close keys ②③ · `except_bank_line` · opening-seed approval · statutory wording
· `canonical` definitions · capability grants · e-filing. **Passing to Clara by the register's own logic,
not by a grant each:** void statement · void reconciliation · resolve exception incl. write-off · the 60-day
stale-item waiver · bank-account registration · opening a client file · abandon and re-freeze a close ·
template publish · the definition lifecycle incl. REJECT · snapshot mint · render-drift consent.

*Grounds the owner gave:* the product's vision is maximum agentic autonomy, and the professional-signature
framing — those seven acts, the ones a professional signs his name to — is what holds the statutory line. A
longer human list does not buy safety; it buys a product that stops.

*Riders kept as design law* (fail-closed, attached by the orchestrator under the standing delegation; they
narrow nothing ruled): **(1)** new authority arrives as **wake SIBLING verbs** — a live human body is never
rewritten to add a lane, so a new grant never needs a D1 write-quiesce window; **(2)** capabilities are
**default-on with no per-firm dial**, re-confirming ADR-0072② — the brake is the act's own reversibility,
not a signature; **(3)** a **mechanical duplicate-payment wall** rides the 60-day waiver, whose whole
function is to make the question "is this a duplicate payment?" disappear.

*ORCHESTRATOR'S DISSENT — option A, the two-question rule — ON FILE.* A recommended a decision RULE rather
than an open register: of each unnamed act ask **(i)** does it make a difference, an obligation or a named
question DISAPPEAR from the books, or make something bearing the firm's name effective outside it (a
certificate, a published template, an issued pack)? and **(ii)** is it STRUCTURALLY IRREVERSIBLE (bytes
sent, a permanent row minted, a human's signature voided)? Either yes → human; both no → Clara. **The costs
stated, in full and in session:** **voiding a reconciliation destroys a segment of reconciliation history
with no human eye on the act** — what is lost is a certification, not a posting, so reverse-not-delete does
not recover it; and **registering a bank account binds an account to a COA code, so a wrong binding
reconciles an entire month into the wrong account while the screen still reads "reconciled"** — the silent
wrong number, which hard constraint 1 ranks above everything else. The dissent priced its own cost equally:
under A the 60-day waiver stays human, real books carry over-60-day open items most months, and unattended
reconciliation would routinely stop and wait. **Heard, costed, declined.** Rider (3) is the wall the
duplicate-payment concern earns inside the owner's ruling.

**(TA-P2) Numbers with no document — RULED: A+.** *The question:* the witness pair governs numbers PRINTED
ON a document; accruals, provisions, depreciation, rates, thresholds, FX and model prices have none — does
law 71's judgement-is-authority reach them?

**RULED: three origins, and only three.** A number entering the books or a formal artifact comes from **(1)**
a witness-pair reading, **(2)** a **versioned deterministic evaluator** recomputing it from data already
posted (depreciation off the FA register, amortisation off the posted prepayment, model spend = tokens × a
versioned price row), or **(3)** a row of an **effective-dated policy table**. None of the three → she DRAFTS
and says honestly that she cannot compute it. **CALCULATIONS are hers to decide and the evaluator's to
produce — automatic, unattended. ESTIMATES (provisions, bad debt) are drafts carrying her rationale**, and
one human approval click makes the figure the human's judgement under law 1's exact-revision clause. **POLICY
TABLES are the A+ half:** she fetches the official sources and **DRAFTS the row**; it lands through an
**audited owner one-click door — NOT a PR** — behind two mechanical checks (two independent official sources
agree; the value sits inside a plausibility band). Rows are **immutable + supersede** (revision ·
`superseded_by` · actor); a backdated effective date **triggers a downstream impact scan** naming affected
entries for a human rather than recomputing silently; **a missing row for the day REFUSES — never carries
the previous row forward.**

*Grounds and alignment:* the owner first leaned B ("Clara can calculate if her context is right"). B's
calculation half is already A; B's estimate half and its carry-forward would amend the cardinal invariant
(law 1 · PRD §6 · hard constraint 2), so he took **A+** — the automation he wanted, bought with a governed
door rather than with the invariant. **The constitution is untouched.** *Riders:* **F-A8-OQ-5** — the SST
rate table is built by the side with a named consumer, **F-T1**; F-A8 only attaches the fetch to a table that
exists. **F-A8-M5** — Tier-1 CLOSES to `fx_rates` + the SST rate table + the SST threshold table; income-tax
bands, capital allowances, EPF/SOCSO/EIS, stamp duty and MTD are out until their own consumers land.

**(TA-P3) Egress governance for a new class of model processing — RULED: A.** *The question:* to decide
WHOSE document this is she must read it, but the rule is that the client must be known and consented BEFORE the read.

**RULED: one processing class = one NAMED purpose.** Every client signs **ONCE at onboarding**, purpose list
complete, and the product activates every listed purpose from that document. For the two **structurally
client-less** moments — the pre-attribution read and the onboarding interview — **BELCORT signs ONCE a
firm-level NARROW purpose**, walled by its output: the read may produce **an attribution verdict or a form
suggestion and nothing else** — never an accounting fact, never an amount — over a **closed
admissible-document list** (SSM/ROB certificate, SST certificate, bank statement or bank letter, LHDN
correspondence, engagement letter; **IC and passport refused**). **Identity-free regulatory lookups are not
disclosures.** **`classify`'s live ungoverned egress comes under the purpose list** (document-processing):
today it ships OCR text to a provider with no consent of any kind, and F-A7a may not be built on it until
that closes. The `fetch` tool's non-public-address deny list (localhost · RFC1918 · fly 6PN · cloud metadata)
is **not** a domain whitelist — law 75's "no whitelist" governs content sources, not egress targets.

*Grounds, from the owner's own question "signed once at signup?":* yes — A = once with the purposes listed;
B = once with purpose "any", legally weak under the PDPA and the MIA By-Laws, which require *specific*
authority. *Consequences accepted:* the **C6 checklist (DPA · written client disclosure · a documented PDPA
cross-border basis) moves from "later" to CRITICAL PATH**, because the firm-level narrow purpose sends
material out at the moment governance is weakest; each new client gains one owner signature step; the three
existing clients need one supplementary line.

**(TA-P4) Receipts, and the strength of a human nod — RULED: A.** *The question:* law 71 wrote receipt
discipline for POSTING only, and where a receipt exists at all the field naming *why* is a sentence the model wrote about itself.

**RULED: receipts bind mechanically, and a human can read them.** **(1)** Law 71's discipline **extends to
every agent judgement act** — freeze, report run, read, web fetch, drafted statement — carrying model +
version + rationale; `via_wake_kind` stops being hard-written NULL. **(2)** The who / why / from-where fields
**bind mechanically to the triggering act** — chat turn, wake task, fetch call — and the model's sentence is
an annotation, never the only evidence. **(3)** Read and receipt commit **in ONE transaction: no receipt, no
read** — a **DEFINER read wrapper**, not a bare read-only connection; it may still take SQL text as a
parameter, so it does not contradict the freeform-read shape. **(4)** A **human read surface at the
`audit_log` bookkeeper+ floor** — today not even the owner can read that table. **The human half:** one
explicit "I have reviewed this" act, the receipt recording **author = Clara** and **whether the text was
adopted verbatim**; batch echo-confirm is allowed, but the fields where one wrong value bends everything
downstream — legal name, entity type, FY end, opening stance, CoA seed — stay per-field. **F-A8's citation
discipline becomes a TOOL-BOUNDARY mechanism:** the fetch tool mints its own citation row (URL + accessed
date + quote), and a receipt whose act called the tool but carries zero citation rows is REFUSED — hard
constraint 2 requires structural enforcement, not a prompt.

*Grounds:* the owner's "this looks troublesome" was answered — ~95% engineering, the human's actions
unchanged but for one review click. *Consequence with a schedule:* the close-side receipt columns touch
**`finalize_close`, the same live body Track B's task #17 Fix A must replace** — one migration or strict
ordering, and **one D1 write-quiesce window**.

**(TA-P5) May a clock wake her? — RULED: A, in PRINCIPLE form.** *The question:* nothing wakes on a date
today, yet month-end reconciliation, close preparation and the monthly pack all want a clock — is that law
71's lane or law 21's calendar-belt doctrine?

**RULED: the system gains ONE time-triggered wake source, and what she does on waking runs under law 71** —
no ramp, no first-run draft, no sampling, exactly as G1.2/G1.3 ruled. Two disciplines ride it. **Data
gating:** a clock means *wake and look*; the WORK still triggers on data — a missing statement produces a
chase notice, never a fabricated reconciliation, and a client who dumps twelve statements at FY end has them
reconciled in chain order on the event. **Receipts:** every clocked act is receipted under TA-P4. *In scope:*
periodic posting belts · reconciliation, close and management-pack initiation · retry-later on ambiguous
lines and failed tasks · document chasing with a per-client mute · scheduled official fetches (law 75) ·
expiry reminders · **statutory-deadline PREPARATION** (SST-02, EPF/SOCSO/PCB, CP204, Form C/B/E, e-Invoice) —
**submission itself stays human** (law 71, unchanged).

**LAW 21 IS NARROWED, IN THE OWNER'S OWN WORDS, TO "PERIODIC POSTING BELTS".** It governs whether a recurring
POSTING carries admin-signed authority and a first-fires-draft ramp; it never governed whether a clock may
wake her, and saying so out loud is the point — unsaid, this lane would later read as an end-run around law
21. *Grounds the owner gave:* law 75 already presumes scheduled fetches, so standing law already contains a
clock; and option B would re-admit through the back door the ramp G1.2/G1.3 refused at the front. **A new
clocked act needs a design cell — what it checks, what it does when data is missing, who is notified — not a
new ruling.** *Design riders:* a visible notice and a human "hold" button on the first calendar-driven lanes;
a new wake kind (a CHECK pair plus six roster surfaces — the D34 live-CHECK swap is the precedent); and
**self-run report packs are exempt from `0084`'s orphan-adoption rule**, or such a pack waits forever.

**(TA-P6) Whom does a two-person wall measure now? — RULED: A.** *The question:* Clara now stands at one end
of walls that compare two human identities, and **the failure is silent — if the agent identity occupies an
arm the wall does not error, it dissolves, and the human who directed the work quietly regains the right to
approve his own request with not one character changed.**

**RULED: the agent identity never occupies either end.** **(1)** Walls **re-aim at the DIRECTING human**
(`on_behalf_of` → `requested_by`; law 69's shape). **(2)** With no director — a self-run — the wall does not
vanish: the issue step **fails closed to a human act by someone who did not prepare the work**. **(3)**
Receipts tell the truth: an agent-prepared year is **`agent_prepared`** ("prepared by Clara; the sole human
signer is X"), never `two_person`. **SOLO firms:** the existing **CLR05 self-attestation arm** (law 4;
live-proven at BEE) is the path, and the **report-issue segregation wall GAINS a solo arm** to match the
close wall's, auto-upgrading to distinct-checker the moment a second human joins. *Consequences:* this
touches `finalize_close` and the receipt CHECK domain — **the same body as task #17 and TA-P4**, so one
migration or strict ordering and **one D1 window** covers all three; the issue-wall re-arm is judgement logic
and takes review law 1's independent pass.

**(TA-P7) May a non-human judgement satisfy invariant (a)? — RULED: C, BY THE OWNER. ATTRIBUTION IS THE
AGENT'S JUDGEMENT; WHEN SHE IS UNSURE SHE CLARIFIES WITH THE USER.** *The question:* the wall deciding which
client a document belongs to accepts exactly two answers today — a human click or an exact identifier match —
and F-A7a cannot exist without a third.

**RULED: option C.** In the owner's words — *"as long as the agentic LLM can browse the right DB/context,
that is enough."* Attribution becomes **the agent's judgement under structural walls, and she clarifies when
unsure** — the posture G1 gave posting. **A CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT, RATIFIED 2026-08-22**; wording below.

*Walls-validate riders* (fail-closed, under the standing delegation; none narrows the ruling): **(1)** a
**hard-number CONTRADICTION wall** — a printed registration mapping to a DIFFERENT client refuses and asks;
judgement may fill a silence, never overrule a registered identifier. **(2)** A **name-family COLLISION
guard** — more than one candidate must clarify; BELCORT's own books carry ROME PROPERTIES and ROME SECRETARY,
and ROME PUBLIC ADVISORY returns as a real counterparty after the Wave-G reset, so "ROME" is a live collision
source on the owner's own data. **(3)** The **correction path plus a named misrouted-egress event** (TA-P3's
F-A7-M2): unposted → she re-attributes; posted → reverse and raise a question. Attribution routes CONSENT, so
it earns a governance receipt, not merely a bookkeeping correction. **(4)** A **firm-scoped question carrier
for the unattributable document** — `wake_open_question` requires an attributed client today, so an
unattributed document cannot by construction reach the fallback; the new scope and its RLS shape ship **with
F-A7a**, or "it falls to a human queue" is an empty promise.

*ORCHESTRATOR'S DISSENT — option A, DB-judged hard-number corroboration — ON FILE.* A would have opened the
wall by one notch: a new "AI-corroborated" attribution method conditioned on **two independent reads (the
OCR-text channel and the original-image channel) recovering the SAME hard identifier**, that identifier
**anchored to a layout region**, pointing to exactly one client in the firm — **with the verdict decided by a
versioned deterministic DB predicate, never a model's self-reported score**, the shape the owner himself
chose for amounts at G1.1. **The costs stated, in full and in session:** **a wrong attribution corrupts TWO
clients' books at once** — the document lands in one and is missing from the other — and once posted,
unwinding means reversing before re-filing; and the **majority of Malaysian SME bills print only a customer
NAME**, no TIN and no SSM, so judgement, not corroboration, carries most of the population — which is exactly
why A would have left a long human queue and why the owner declined it. The dissent priced its own cost with
equal candour: under A that queue is permanent, and "throw the document in and it books itself" would be
false for the common case. **Heard, costed, declined.** Riders (1) and (2) are what the collision argument
earns inside the owner's ruling. *The briefed option C's third form — a model-self-reported confidence score
— was excluded at the agenda and is NOT what was ruled:* it is isomorphic to law 72's prohibition on a model
grading its own agreement. **What was ruled is judgement under walls, never scores.**

**(TA-P8) May what she learns become tomorrow's authority? — RULED: B in general form; the RS-specific
extension DROPPED; a promotion door GRANTED.** *The question:* bank matching, attribution and web lookups all
bring back identifiers — may any of them become an exact-match KEY?

**RULED: identifiers Clara learns by JUDGEMENT are recorded in the knowledge layer as CONTEXT for her next
judgement, never written as exact-match keys.** Keys come from exactly two places: **(i)** human
confirmation, or **(ii)** a printed identifier read identically by BOTH witness channels. **The promotion
door — the owner asked for it, and it is granted:** Clara may PROPOSE "promote this to a hard fact" as a
typed proposal card (identifier seen N times, judgement stable); **one human click** writes the key through
the audited counterparty door (`0063`'s shape), and the receipt records who confirmed it. **Hard constraint
12 stays pinned to ROME SECRETARY** and rides to the Wave-G reset — NOT extended to all external identifiers;
what survives generally is the principle that matters: **a name-only counterparty is never enriched by
inference.** *Grounds the owner gave:* RS is a test-era artifact and future clients will also arrive with
incomplete data, so the rule must be general, not RS-shaped. He also asked why the system carries so many
hardcodes and rule machines; answered — **the rule machines RETIRE, the last at TA-P11**, and what remains
are **walls that validate**, not rules that decide.

**(TA-P9) Where may a free read reach? — RULED: A.** *The question:* the read-only role F-A6 would run on can
SELECT almost the whole ledger — seventy migrations of accumulation nobody reviewed **as a decision** — and
isolates only to the FIRM, which is audit finding **GAP5-5 (HIGH), unclaimed**.

**RULED: the read surface is a DECIDED surface, and there is no signature gate.** **(1)** A free read from a
client-bound session is **server-side scoped to that client** — the range compiled into the query, not asked
of the prompt. **(2)** A cross-client read is a **NAMED, receipted action** — answered, not refused. **(3)**
The **HOME chat (no client pinned) is firm-wide by design** — that is where the "global" feel lives; **never
cross-firm** (invariant (c), untouched). **(4)** The readable table list is **enumerated and printed as an
audit line** (law 34), never inherited from accumulated grants — this closes GAP5-5. **(5)** Only the
`interactive` wake at first; unattended lanes use typed reads until a separate ruling, and the wake allowlist
is named as **structurally unable** to reach a bare SELECT, so this boundary is enforced by TA-P4's read
wrapper and nothing else. **(6)** No per-asker RBAC tiering (none of the six typed readers has any) and no
per-firm signature gate (TA-P1's default-on). *Grounds:* the owner's UX concern — "no global access feel,
like ChatGPT?" — was answered: the experience is unchanged; the scoping is engineering the user never sees.

**(TA-P10) How far may the non-authoritative layer travel? — RULED: C′ (the owner leaned C; this is the
aligned form).** *The question:* may a watermarked sandbox output become a file, leave the building, cross
clients — and is an ad-hoc free-query total "a DB-owned number"?

**RULED:** **(1)** Sandbox outputs **EXPORT freely, watermark burned into the BYTES** (not a CSS layer), with
an export record. **(2)** **CROSS-CLIENT exports are allowed when a mechanical check passes — the recipient
must cover every `client_id` in the file** — so a group owner may receive a multi-company comparison; a
covered-recipient test, not the blanket ban the briefing offered. **(3)** Watermark wording is a **versioned
claim-policy row the owner signs ONCE in three languages** (E-R14's governance); **code carries no default
string, and a missing row refuses the render.** **(4)** A free-query aggregate is **NARRATIVE**: she may state
it, chart it, export it and **cite it as a reasoning input in a receipt** with the query text included (law
75's citation shape) — but it **never becomes an authoritative number in a durable artifact**: not a posting
amount, not a formal report cell, not a knowledge-base fact. Those come only from TA-P2's three origins.
*Grounds:* "what does 'not into authoritative artifacts' mean?" was answered on the owner's own books — BEE's
half-year sales figure computed ad hoc is not reproducible from a versioned definition; a formal report cell
must be. *Not taken:* C's fourth element, treating free-SQL aggregates as ordinary DB reads, would amend PRD
§6.1. *Rider:* the sandbox render path is a SECOND entrance to rendering; it shares one geometry library and
is watched in review under TA-P11's own test.

**(TA-P11) What counts as "a second architecture"? — RULED: A — one test and three answers.** *The question:*
G1.4 named the INVOICE rules machine for retirement, but the BANK side has its own human-signed rules machine
that nobody has ever named, and F-A2 put it out of scope.

**THE TEST, RULED:** *a shared deterministic core with one entrance per surface is ONE architecture; two
mutually-unaware computations of the same fact, or two mutually-unaware authority paths to it, are TWO.* By
that test: **(1)** the **bank rules machine RETIRES WHOLE** — propose/sign/retire bank rules, the sightings
table and its three-sighting breeding, `accept_bank_rule_suggestion`, and the four dashboard surfaces: writes
stop, history rows stay as knowledge fuel, the surfaces come down; the treatment D35/D36 gave the invoice
side. **(2)** **7A-R3 ("a no-tax invoice never posts unattended") and E-R13's registered mechanical lift are
FORMALLY RECORDED AS DISSOLVED with the machine they rode** — "no tax printed" is no longer an extra gate.
The successor wall is the witness pair's nil-tax arm plus the SST-registrant lock (opener ②, lock 3); the
genuine residual — a client who *ought* to charge SST but issues no-tax invoices — belongs to F-T1's SST
engine, not to a posting gate. **This is a RECORD, not a silence:** ADR-0072① forbids a law lapsing by
inertia, and without this paragraph 7A-R3 would have gone void the moment F-A2 merged, unannounced. **(3)**
**SST-02 gets its own form producer**, outside the seal/claim chain but **sharing the deterministic
evaluators and the bigint arithmetic beneath** — by the test that is not a second architecture, and F-A10's
closing criterion now says so in writing.

*Ruled BEFORE PR-1 merges, as the agenda asked* — after that moment consequence (2) is in force in fact
whether or not anyone wrote it down. *Cost accepted:* ROME SECRETARY's twenty-two all-no-tax invoices begin
posting unattended the moment F-A2 lands, and if a client's SST registration status is judged wrong the error
runs in the "should have charged tax, treated as no-tax" direction — a tax-filing problem that posts quietly.
**F-A10 can now close.**

**(TA-P12) The brake census — RULED: A.** *The question:* G8 ruled meter-never-cap, yet live code still
refuses on usage in at least three places.

**RULED: one census, one adjudication, two execution batches.** **REMOVE:** the chat lane's daily token hard
cap (a Slice-4 artifact) · the unattended lane's `refused_budget` refusal at 60% and 100% of the daily budget
— **the exact "automation paused by budget" G8 names** · the 15-unattended-sales-drafts-per-day quota, a 7A

**HOTFIX ahead of F-A9**, being a live behaviour already in violation of a ruling; the unattended gate ships
with F-A9 or with that lane's own retirement — **the owner accepted that this work may be wasted rather than
let an in-violation behaviour stand for weeks.** **A RENAME IS MANDATORY:** engine-protection refusals share
the receipt string `refused_budget` with budget refusals, so once the budget gate is gone a pure engine
refusal would still print "insufficient budget" in a receipt a human reads. History rows are append-only and
keep the old spelling; read surfaces explain both. PRD open question 3's "budgets" wording rides the sweep.

**(TA-P13) One metering ledger or two? — RULED: A.** *The question:* two substrates are live, and the new one
is append-only — a dimension not recorded today can never be recovered.

**RULED: ONE ledger of record.** **(1)** `llm_usage_events` becomes the sole ledger, reshaped to hold ANY call
kind — the two mandatory foreign keys become nullable and a call-kind discriminator is added; every new
feature (F-A2, F-A6, F-A7b, F-A8) records through this one door. **(2)** **`client_id` and the triggering
actor are added NOW, nullable** — the irreversible half: on an append-only table, months recorded without

Malaysian firm's profitability turns on. **(3)** The Slice-4 daily/per-task ledger and its reserve/reconcile
machinery **RETIRE once TA-P12's gates are gone. This deletes live real data, and the owner's choice of A is
recorded as exactly that sentence** — ADR-060's authority is DATA-scoped and the schema retirement rides its

gun. **(4)** No cross-firm operator UI this time, but the underlying model is not hard-wired to a single
firm. *Harness-sweep item:* digest **law 76 says "per-call LLM usage"; the source (ADR-0071/G8 and contract
F-A9) says "per-call usage"** with no qualifier — a digest drift, trued below.

**(TA-P14) What does DONE mean for a Wave-F item? — RULED: A, closed-loop delivery.** *The question:* Clara
gets close key ① while keys ②③ have no door anywhere, "she proposes the close" has no carrier, three year-end
gates are vacuously green, "end to end" stops at a human act, the renderer's seven-year promise has never
been drilled, and two findings have lost their content.

**RULED: an item is done when the loop it opens can be walked end to end by whoever must walk it.** Six
clauses. **(1)** It **repairs the gates its own output feeds** — the drawer-2 bank gate to **F-A3** by
measurement origin, the uncoded-voucher gate to **F-A4**, drawer-1's P-3 staying with **F-T4**; only the
producing side knows a gate's measurement basis, and dual claiming means two lanes editing one piece of
judgement logic. **(2)** It **builds the MINIMAL door for every human act it manufactures** — finalize,
abandon, a "Clara proposes close" review card, the signed-original archive verb. **The UI may be crude; it
may not be absent.** **(3)** A proposal has a **durable carrier** — the open-question closed worlds extended
only-widening (D34 precedent), or a close-proposal object. **(4)** **Acceptance runs on REAL books**; where
none exists — BEE's FY2025 is deferred wholesale to the Wave-G reset — a full synthetic round labelled per
ADR-048, **with the deferral RECORDED**: on the record, never an omission. **(5)** F-A5's "end to end" is
**rewritten open→evaluate→seal→render, issue stated as a human act.** **(6)** **Lost records are treated as
lost** — N2 and N4 re-scanned at their items' design stage, rediscoveries registered anew, old ids retired.
*Renderer clause:* **the first real seal plus a byte-reproduction drill — closing DR-render's unrun boundary
— comes BEFORE N3's chart work**; every renderer image is retained seven years (E-R14); a renderer change
