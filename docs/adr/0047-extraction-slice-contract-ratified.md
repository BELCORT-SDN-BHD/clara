### ADR-047 — The extraction-slice contract RATIFIED v1.0 (owner grilling, 2026-07-27)
**Decision:** `docs/plan/extraction-slice-contract.md` moves DRAFT v0.1 → **v1.0 LAW** after the owner ruled its four open questions plus the dependency branch they opened. **(1)** X5 drops Azure's vendor self-reported confidence from gating **entirely** — `corroborated` = two independent readers agree to the sen ∧ the document's own arithmetic identity ∧ the polygon wall ∧ MYR; confidence survives only as diagnostic metadata, and reader disagreement is a refusal, never a tie to adjudicate. **(2)** `request_reextraction` ships at the **bookkeeper** floor (the draft proposed admin; the owner widened it) — audited, op-key idempotent. **(3)** Re-extraction cost policy: **no per-document cap, audit only** (the draft offered a cap of 3) — the structural bound is that the verb is **human-invoked only**: no workflow, sweep, or machine caller may enqueue it, ever; churn cannot mint authority because corroboration is deterministic agreement and posting still passes human approval. **(4)** `vendor_unresolved` — the auto-draft lane's second 29/29 blocker — is **diagnosed AND fixed in-slice**, data + logic fixes both pre-authorized (the draft proposed diagnose-only), under two absolute constraints: the fix is its own block (**never** inside X5's micro-migration; X5 stays LAST and ALONE) and a resolution-logic change carries its own cross-model adversarial review before merge; XG3 widens to name which blocker fell and what fix was applied (new component **X6**). Engineering shapes recorded at ratification: X3's component taxonomy is a **closed enumeration read off the document's face** (subtotal · service charge · discount · delivery/handling · tax · rounding) with the tie *sum-of-stated-components = stated total exactly, else refuse* — every failure mode a refusal, never a wrong post; and a rig verification (not assumption) that an open draft binds its specific extraction version so a mid-review re-extraction cannot swap figures under an approver.
**Why:** The gate that opens posting authority must not rest on a self-reported model score (measured 0/29 ≥ 0.95, max 0.837); agreement between independent readers is the same corroboration shape the XML tier already uses, and dropping the term entirely means no bar exists to ever lower — the standing "never lower 0.95" law is honoured by construction. The bookkeeper floor and no-cap rulings put the verb where the work happens and price the per-page Azure cost (~RM0.05) as noise, keeping the bound structural (human-invoked-only) instead of budget machinery without a problem. Fix-in-slice acknowledges the slice's purpose: the first production draft is unreachable while EITHER blocker stands, and a slice that leaves the second blocker for later cannot demonstrate its own gate (XG3).
